NaapKar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Summary

The short version

NaapKar is a measurement book for tailors. It works completely offline. Your customers, measurements, orders and photos are stored only on your phone. We do not run a server that holds your shop data, and we never sell anything to anyone.

1. Who we are

NaapKar ("the app") is developed and published by the NaapKar team. For any privacy question, contact us at the email address at the bottom of this page.

2. What stays on your phone

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded by us:

  • Customer names, phone numbers and notes
  • Measurements for every garment type
  • Orders, delivery dates, totals and payments
  • Accessory records such as lace, buttons and lining
  • Reference photos and customer photos
  • Your settings: language, unit, theme and shop name

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted with it. Create a backup file first if you want to keep it.

3. Photos

When you add a photo, the app uses the Android system photo picker. You choose one image and only that image is copied into the app's private folder. The app does not request storage or gallery permission and cannot see any other photo on your phone.

Photos are reduced in size before saving and are not included in the backup file. They are never uploaded.

5. Advertising

The free version may show ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising identifier to limit repeated ads and to measure performance. You can reset or delete this identifier in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Google's own policy applies to that data and can be read at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

NaapKar Pro removes all ads.

6. Permissions

The app requests one permission only:

  • Internet — used for Google Sign-In, Play Billing and ads. Everything else works offline.

The app does not request contacts, location, SMS, call logs, camera or external storage.

7. Children

NaapKar is a business tool for tailors and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Deleting your data

Data on your phone

Open Settings → Delete all data inside the app, or uninstall the app.

Your account

If you signed in with Google, open Settings → Delete account in the app. You can also request deletion by email and we will complete it within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Significant changes will also be described in the app's Play Store release notes.
